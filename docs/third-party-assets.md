# Third-party 3D assets

## Vitruvian digital human

`public/models/vitruvian-body.glb`, `vitruvian-head.glb` and the `vit-*.webp`
textures are adapted from
[ibrews/VitruvianGodot](https://github.com/ibrews/VitruvianGodot).

- The Vitruvian/CharMorph character, morphs and textures are CC0.
- The source project's code is MIT.
- Animation clips in the body GLB were retargeted from Mixamo and remain subject
  to Adobe's Mixamo terms. SHOPan uses the `Idle` clip for the fitting-room pose.
- The WebP files are 1024px derivatives of the source PNG textures, produced to
  keep browser loading practical.

See the upstream `LICENSE` and `NOTICE.md` before redistributing these assets.

## SMPL
